# 2.17 No seams: no lines along triangle and quad edges

**Goal:** no lines along triangle and quad edges. The user's 5K screenshot (taken before 2.16, at XYZ 57.60 232.67 −4087.75, yaw 257.7, pitch −18.8) shows lines along the edges like a faint wireframe. The 2.16 look shots show faint seam lines at the dirt block edges near spawn. Remove them without visibly changing anything else and without costing frame time.

**Accept:** no crack or edge line is visible in the look shots or in the user's view at 5K; frame time is unchanged; the user approves the look.

## Reproduce

- `far_metrics::look_shots` (1280×720) and `far_metrics::perf_views` with `JBM_PERF_SHOTS=1` (the user's view at 2560×1440, at 5120×2880 native and at 5120×2880 auto) on the 2.16 branch. These are saved under `target/2.17-shots/before/`.
- A new probe, `far_metrics::seam_probe` (ignored), looks for real cracks: places where the sky shows through solid terrain. Each view and size renders twice, the whole sky overridden to magenta and then to green, with fog, water shading and clouds off. A pixel that differs between the two shows the sky in at least one sample. The 8-connected components of those pixels that do not touch the image border are cracks.

## Root causes, with evidence

1. **MSAA 4× edge pixels sample the texture on the wrong side of the tile.** This causes the seam lines in the look shots.
   - With MSAA a pixel is shaded once, at its center, even when only some of its samples lie inside the triangle. At a quad's edge that center can lie outside the quad, so the interpolated UV runs slightly past the face's extent.
   - The block sampler repeats, so a UV just past an edge reads the far side of the tile. A grass side's bottom edge reads the green top of the tile, which draws a green line under every grass terrace. A dirt face's edge reads the opposite dirt row.
   - Evidence: the lines appear only at 4× and are absent at `JBM_MSAA=1`. They sit exactly on quad edges. They are the color of the tile's opposite edge.
   - The regression test below measures it. It renders a grass-topped dirt wall at 1× and 4× and checks that pixels whose 3×3 neighbourhood is dirt at 1× are still dirt at 4×. Without the fix the test fails, with green lines along the wall's quad edges.
   - The 2.16 render scale makes this worse at 5K. The scene renders at 2698×1518 and the Catmull-Rom composite magnifies each one-pixel line to about two display pixels.
2. **The user's wireframe look comes from the wireframe debug view.**
   - The wireframe view (`JBM_WIREFRAME=1`, or the F3 panel) darkens every triangle edge, including each quad's diagonal, so it draws lines along both triangle and quad edges.
   - Rendering the user's view at 5K with it on (`target/2.17-shots/probe/user_5k_wireframe.png`) and downscaling it reproduces the screenshot's pattern. That pattern includes diagonals, which no texture seam can draw.
   - With the view off, the same frame has no diagonal lines.
   - Nothing in the frame said a debug view was on. The HUD now says so (see the fix below).
3. **Not a cause: T-junction or AO-diagonal cracks in the meshed geometry.**
   - The probe finds **no cracks inside the near terrain** at any size: 1280×720, 2560×1440, 5120×2880 native and 5120×2880 auto.
   - The few components it does find lie along the far horizon, where an LOD level's border crosses a ridge. One example is the 1-pixel strip under a far ridge in `target/seam-probe/user_5120x2880.png`.
   - A test changed `EDGE_EXPAND` to scale with distance, which would close any T-junction gap a few ulps wide. The crack counts did not change, so these are not rounding cracks. They are LOD-border pinholes: a coarse level's sampled surface dips below the finer terrain, and the skirts cover only the top two cells. That is a geometry and LOD issue, and it is left out of scope (see below).
   - AO is interpolated per vertex across each quad's diagonal, but the quad is split along the AO-preferred diagonal, and with the wireframe off no line follows it.

## The fix

1. **A per-face UV clamp in the terrain fragment shader** (`fix(client)`).
   - The vertex shader passes the face's UV extent as a flat `uv_max`.
   - The fragment shader clamps the UV to `[UV_EDGE_INSET, uv_max − UV_EDGE_INSET]` and then calls the implicit `textureSample`. `UV_EDGE_INSET` is 1/1024 of a tile: 1/64 of a texel, and still a distinct float at the largest UV a face reaches (32 cells of 64 blocks).
   - Inside the face the clamp does nothing, so only pixels at quad edges change. The diagonals keep the exact UV.
   - Rejected alternatives:
     - **Centroid interpolation** also moves edge samples inside the triangle, but its derivatives jump along every triangle edge, diagonals included. That shifted the mip level along the diagonals: the mesh_ao and mesh_blocks goldens failed with diffs scattered along them.
     - **`textureSampleGrad`** with the unclamped derivatives passed the goldens but moved interior pixels slightly everywhere.
   - **No vertex output added.** The terrain pass is vertex-bound on the M5 Pro's tile-based GPU, so each per-vertex output costs. An extra `vec2` varying alone cost about 0.3 ms at 2560×1440, consistently across A/B runs. Layer, level and direction now share one flat `face: u32` (layer bits 0..16, level 16..20, direction 20..23), which frees the two slots `uv_max` needs. With that, the cost is in the noise (table below).
   - The mesher is untouched: faces and commands are identical (808 797 faces and 5842 commands at the user's view).
2. **The HUD names the active debug views** (`feat(client)`). `RenderSettings::debug_view_names()` lists every view that is not at its default (`WIREFRAME` or `WIREFRAME-ONLY`, `CHUNK-BORDERS`, `EXPLODE n`, `LEVELS …`, `FREEZE-CULL`, `NO-FOG`, `NO-SUN`, `NO-CLOUDS`, `NO-WATER`). The HUD shows them as a `VIEWS …` line, so a screenshot with the wireframe on says so.
3. **The seam probe** (`test(game)`) is kept as an ignored tool and documented in the game README.

**Regression test:** `crates/client/tests/look.rs::quad_edges_draw_no_texture_seam_at_4x` builds the grass-topped dirt wall, renders it at 1× and 4×, and checks every pixel whose 1× 3×3 neighbourhood is dirt (more than 2000 pixels). It fails on the old shader and passes on the new one.

## Before and after

**Crops** (before on the left, after on the right, nearest-upscaled), under `target/2.17-shots/crops/`:
- `spawn_eye_seams.png`: the green line under the grass edge and the line along the dirt quad's top edge are gone.
- `spawn_60up_seams.png`: the green line under each terrace is gone.
- `mountain_seams.png`: the far grassland and slopes. Only the step edges' pixels change, and the change is hardly visible at this distance.
- `user_5k_auto.png` and `user_5k_native.png`: the user's view at 5K, auto and native. With the wireframe off, no line is visible before or after. The dark contours are terrace steps, not seams.

The full frames are in `target/2.17-shots/{before,after}/` (`look/` for the five look sites with and without tint, plus `user_{2560x1440,5120x2880,5120x2880@1}.png`).

**Frame time.** `perf_views`, the user's view, cpu-cull, MSAA 4×, M5 Pro. Each run settles the world once, then measures three rounds at 2560×1440 and two at 5120×2880 auto. Every variant ran twice. GPU is min / p50 in ms: the lowest min across the runs, and the mean p50 across all rounds.

| Variant | 2560×1440 | 5120×2880 auto (2698×1518) | CPU p50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (2.16) | 3.83 / 4.60 | 5.07 / 6.35 | 0.86–0.95 |
| **after: clamp + packed `face`** | 3.86 / 4.62 | 5.31 / 6.46 | 0.85–1.00 |

The packed version is within 0.02 ms of 2.16 at 2560×1440 and within 0.11 ms at 5K. That is well inside the run's round-to-round spread at 5K (about 1.8 ms between the first and second round of the same build). Faces, commands and draws are unchanged.

**Goldens regenerated.** All four changed only along quad edges. I read each golden, actual and diff image before regenerating:
- `crates/client/tests/golden/mesh_blocks.png` (0.54 % of pixels): the block row's and the floor's quad outlines. The edge pixels no longer read the opposite side of the tile.
- `crates/client/tests/golden/edit_spawn.png` (1.68 %): the grass top's quad outlines and the dug hole's edges.
- `crates/client/tests/golden/terrain_spawn.png` (2.37 %): the green line along every terrace step.
- `crates/client/tests/golden/rings_corner3.png` (0.63 %): sub-pixel terrace sides in the near ring.

`rings_border12` and `rings_close` also moved slightly, but they stayed within the tolerance and were not rewritten. No other golden changed.

## Left out

- **The LOD-border pinholes at the far horizon** (the probe's remaining components). They are a geometry and LOD issue: a coarse level's sampled peaks dip below the finer terrain where the level border crosses a ridge, and the skirts cover only the top two cells. Fixing them would change meshing or skirts, and they show only at the horizon with fog off. A later LOD task should take them, with `seam_probe` as the measurement.
- **The composite upscale** is unchanged. It only magnified the edge seam, and with the seam gone there is nothing left for it to magnify.
