# 2.15 The look: sun light, sky, water, anti-aliasing, haze

**Goal:** the game looks clean and finished from the first frame. Today (see `cargo test --release -p jbm-game --test far_metrics look_shots -- --ignored --nocapture`, written to `target/look-shots/`):
- terrain is unlit flat color with fixed per-direction multipliers;
- the ocean is one flat paint-bucket blue;
- the sky is a bare gradient with no sun;
- every edge is jagged (no anti-aliasing);
- distant grass hills read as high-contrast brown contour stripes, because terrace sides are dirt-brown and nothing softens them.

The user's verdict: "very bad and unprofessional". After this task the same five views must read as a polished voxel game: sunlit terrain with soft ambient, a sky with a sun and clouds, water that looks like water, smooth edges, and distance that fades into atmospheric haze.

## Scope

### A. Sun and sky light (all levels, all paths)

- A fixed sun: no day cycle yet. Pick a pleasant mid-morning direction, roughly 35–55° elevation, not axis-aligned, so all four sides of a block read differently. Use a warm-white sun color and a hemisphere ambient: sky blue from above, a dimmer warm ground bounce from below.
- Add these to `FrameUniforms` (mirror every WGSL copy and the layout test).
- Terrain fragment color:
  ```
  albedo · (ambient(n) · ao + sun · max(dot(n, L), 0) · mix(1, ao, k))
  ```
  where `n` is the face normal from the direction. Delete the fixed `face_shade` table.
- Tune it so a sunlit grass top is about as bright as today's (no washed-out whites, no crushed shadow sides). Shadow sides must still clearly show block shapes.
- The same lighting applies at every LOD level, so LOD0 and L1+ match at the border. Keep the per-level AO softening.
- No shadow maps (out of scope).

### B. Sky

- `sky.wgsl`: a sun disk with a soft glow around it, in the sun's direction.
- A static, deterministic procedural cloud layer: soft 2D-noise clouds on a plane ~200+ blocks up, intersected per sky pixel. They thin out toward the horizon and are drawn only above it.
  - Clouds may drift with a time uniform in the app.
  - Tests render with time 0.
- Fog must still match the sky where terrain meets it. Put the sun glow in a function shared by the sky and the terrain fog, so fogged terrain in the sun's direction blends into the glow. Clouds stay sky-only, since they are above the horizon and terrain fog fades out under them.
- **Do not change the below-horizon branch's behavior when the sky colors are overridden.** The sentinel hole tests set the ground color to magenta and must keep working unchanged.

### C. Water

Water stays an opaque block in the opaque pass: translucency and a separate pass are out of scope. Make it look like water in the fragment shader:
- deep-water base color;
- a Fresnel blend toward the sky color reflected about the surface normal (use `sky_gradient` and the shared sun glow);
- a sun specular highlight;
- subtle procedural normal ripples from the world position (deterministic; animated by the time uniform in the app only).

The same look applies to water at every LOD level: LOD water tops use the same function with the ripple fading out by distance. The seam between LOD0 water and L1+ water must be invisible. Water faces are identified by their texture layer: pass a per-layer "water" flag through a small per-layer material buffer, next to `layer_avg`, not a hard-coded layer number.

### D. Anti-aliasing

MSAA 4× on the window and on the offscreen rig.
- The sky, terrain, debug lines and outline render into a multisampled color + depth target that resolves into the surface or offscreen view. The overlay and egui draw after the resolve at 1×.
- Hi-Z (`hiz.rs`, `hiz.wgsl`) reads the multisampled depth, taking the min over samples. gpu-cull keeps working.
- Use `alpha_to_coverage` for cutout layers (leaves, glass edges), or keep the discard if that is cleaner; say which.
- Make the sample count a setting (`RenderSettings::msaa_samples`, env `JBM_MSAA=1|4`, default 4). It is applied at startup; no live switch.
- `read_depth` and tests that need single-sample depth (`surface_windows.rs`) may run at 1×. Keep them working.
- Path identity (`terrain_paths.rs`, `lod_rings.rs`, `debug_views.rs`) must stay exact at 4×.
- Close-up textures must stay crisp, nearest-magnified pixels. Do not blur them.

### E. Far look and haze

- Replace the plain distance fog with height-aware aerial perspective:
  - extinction plus in-scatter toward the sky/horizon color;
  - thicker low in valleys, thinner at altitude;
  - the shared sun glow in the sun's direction.
- Keep a hard end at `FOG_END_FRACTION`, so the outermost ring edge stays hidden.
- Keep the LOD0 texture-to-flat fade.
- **Contour stripes:** at mid distance (fade > 0 and on L1+), terrace side faces of grass columns must not read as high-contrast brown stripes. Use the lighting and haze from A and E plus a far side color: for layers whose top band differs from the body (grass_side), bias the far side color toward the top material by fade. That is decided per layer in the material buffer, not by a hard-coded layer number. Judge it on the `mountain`, `spawn_60up` and `high_300` shots.
- The LOD0 → L1 border must stay invisible (`lod0_lod1_border_is_invisible_with_flat_colors` keeps passing; restate its numbers if the lighting model needs it).

### F. Panel and settings

`RenderSettings` and debug-panel toggles:
- sun lighting on/off (off is the old flat look, for comparison);
- clouds on/off;
- water shading on/off.

Env vars as for the existing views. MSAA is shown in the panel as read-only.

## Tests

- **`tests/far_look.rs`:**
  - The flat-average check derives its expected value from the new lighting formula (or runs with lighting off).
  - The fog check is restated for the new haze, including a test that fog at `FOG_END_FRACTION` equals the sky behind it.
- **Sky:** sun disk pixels are brighter than the surrounding sky; clouds never draw below the horizon (pixel test); the below-horizon override still returns exactly the override color.
- **Water:** a water surface viewed at a grazing angle is closer to the sky color than one viewed straight down. Pixel test, deterministic at time 0.
- **MSAA:** an edge pixel of a terrain silhouette against the sky is a blend (not either pure color) at 4× and pure at 1×. Path identity is exact at 4×. gpu-cull Hi-Z still culls the cave scene to ≥ 80 %.
- **Goldens:** regenerate every golden that changes (sky, mesh, rings, terrain_spawn, edit_spawn) with `JBM_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`, inspect each one zoomed in, and list them in the report with one line each on what changed. `triangle` must not change.
- **Hole tests:** the sentinel tests (`lod_rings`, `surface_windows`, `lod_fly`) pass with tolerances not grown.
- **All draw paths:** `JBM_NO_MDI=1` and `JBM_GPU_CULL=1` variants of `cargo test -p jbm-client` and `-p jbm-game`.

## Out of scope

- Translucent water or any blended terrain pass.
- Shadow maps.
- Day/night cycle.
- HDR / bloom.
- New block textures.
- Mesher or face-format changes (2.14).
- LOD data.
